## ZAZ Autumn 4.5 FM26: Overview

**ZAZ Autumn 4.5** is a high-intensity, three-at-the-back tactic released by **ZaZ** for **FM26 Patch 26.3.0** in June 2026. It's the tactic he considers his **highest-rated on FM-Arena**, and it has been downloaded over **12,000 times** since launch.

The headline numbers come from a **10,800-match test** on FM-Arena:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|

| Goal difference | **+53** |
| Goals scored / conceded | 100 / 47 |

The core identity: **high pressing, goals from inside the box, and assists mostly through the middle** — with goals and assists spread across the whole squad instead of one star player.

## The 3-4-2-1 Shape

```
                    SS (Shadow Striker)
           IF (Inside Forward)      IF (Inside Forward)

    AWB (Attacking WB)  CHM (Roaming)  CHM (Roaming)  AWB (Attacking WB)

                  OCB        BCB        OCB

                          GK
```

> Role labels follow the FM-Arena release. The exact duty mix can differ slightly between the main and Light versions — check the downloaded file for the precise setup.

Key roles and their instructions from the release:

| Role | Instructions |
|---|---|
| **GK** | Shorter distribution |
| **BCB** (centre ball-playing CB) | Shorter passing, press more |
| **OCB** (outside CBs) | Shoot less, more risky passes, shorter passing, press less |
| **AWB** (attacking wing-backs) | Narrow positioning, cross from byline, shoot less, press more |
| **CHM** (central midfielders) | Roam from position |
| **IF** (inside forwards) | Stay wider, press more |
| **SS** (shadow striker) | Move into channels, press more |
| **Whole team** | **Tackle harder** |

The balance is interesting: the back three build out short and stay calm, while the wing-backs and front four do the aggressive work. That contrast is why it stays solid despite the high pressing line.

## Team Instructions: What Makes It Tick

The FM-Arena page summarises the approach as **"neutral defensive line behaviour with pressing more in the frontline"** — a deliberately aggressive, high-intensity style.

What this means in practice:

- **High press, higher line** — the front line triggers the press; the neutral defensive line keeps the back three from getting dragged out of shape
- **Tackle harder across the team** — wins the ball back quickly in the opponent's half
- **Goals inside the box** — the SS and IFs work the channels and penalty area rather than shooting from distance
- **Through-the-middle assists** — the roaming midfielders (CHM) create overloads centrally, with the wing-backs providing width

The main version is demanding. The **Light version** drops mentality to Positive, uses **Stay On Feet**, and takes **one step down in both Trigger Press and Tempo** — ideal for protecting leads or managing tired squads.

## Player Requirements

ZaZ's attribute priorities are very clear:

### Priority 1: Pace and Acceleration

> "Pace and Acceleration are far more important than any other attribute."

For the wing-backs, IFs and SS this is non-negotiable. The whole system relies on recovering space after the press is beaten.

### Minimums for Everyone

| Attribute | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Work Rate | 6 |
| Agility | 6 |
| Composure | 6 |
| Stamina | 6 |

### Secondary Priorities

- **Jumping Reach** — especially for the back three
- **Balance, Dribbling** — especially for wide players
- **Decisions, Anticipation** — for the press timing

An ideal wide player template from the author's own scouting notes: **Pace 18+, Acceleration 18+, Work Rate 10, Jumping Reach 10+, Dribbling 10+, Balance 10+**.

## Training & Fitness Management

This is where ZAZ Autumn 4.5 differs from most download tactics — the author ships a full support package:

1. **Training schedule** — Rebalance, Growth and Set Pieces schedules included with the download
2. **Super recovery** — schedule **three rest days after every match** to trigger it
3. **Individual focus** — **Quickness** for outfield players; **Agility / Reactions** for goalkeepers
4. **Match-week variants** — use the **"General — 1 Match"** file in single-match weeks and the **"General — 2 Matches"** file in double-game weeks
5. **No opposition instructions** — the author explicitly advises against setting them unless you really know what you're doing
