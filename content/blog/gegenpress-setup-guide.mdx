## What is Gegenpressing?

Gegenpressing (German for "counter-pressing") is the tactical philosophy of winning the ball back immediately after losing it. Instead of retreating into defensive shape, your forwards, midfielders, and even defenders swarm the opponent while they're still disorganized from winning possession.

Jurgen Klopp popularized it at Dortmund and Liverpool. In FM26, it remains **the single most effective tactical approach** in community testing — the top-rated tactic on FM-Arena for three consecutive patches.

---

## The Three Pillars of a Working Gegenpress

### 1. The Right Formation

The **4-2-3-1 with two DMs** is the meta formation for gegenpressing in FM26. Here's why:

- **Two DMs** protect the backline when your front four are pressing high
- **AMC (No. 10)** acts as the first line of press, harassing opposition center-backs
- **Wingers** cut off passing lanes to full-backs
- **Lone striker** presses the goalkeeper and center-backs

Alternative formations that work:
- 4-3-3 (with DM) — better wide coverage
- 4-4-2 — classic pressing, but 4-2-3-1 is more aggressive

### 2. The Right Players

Gegenpress fails without the right personnel. **You MUST have:**

| Attribute | Minimum Value | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Work Rate | 14+ | Players without this won't press hard enough |
| Stamina | 14+ | You'll run 10-15% more per match than a normal tactic |
| Determination | 13+ | Players keep pressing even when losing |
| Tackling (for DMs) | 14+ | They need to actually win the ball after pressing |
| Acceleration | 14+ | Close down quickly before the opponent escapes |

> **Critical**: If your squad averages below 13 Work Rate, **do not play gegenpress**. Use a mid-block instead. An unfit team playing gegenpress concedes 2-4 goals in the last 20 minutes of every match.

## Complete Team Instructions

### In Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Attacking Width | Fairly Narrow | Compact shape aids counter-pressing after turnover |
| Passing Directness | Shorter Passing | Keep the ball when you win it, don't give it straight back |
| Tempo | Slightly Higher | Move the ball quickly while defense is unsettled |
| Play Out of Defence | ✓ Enabled | Don't hoof it — build from the back systematically |
| Pass Into Space | — | Leave off; possession is more important than through balls here |
| Run At Defence | — | Your wingers will do this naturally with the right roles |
| Work Ball Into Box | ✓ Enabled | Reduce low-quality shots from distance |

### In Transition

| Instruction | Setting | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Counter-Press | ✓ Always | This IS gegenpressing. Non-negotiable. |
| Counter | — | Not needed — you'll already be high up the pitch |
| GK Distribution | Roll It Out | Distribute to Centre-Backs — build from clean transitions |
| Distribute Quickly | — | Not necessary; trust your CBs to progress the ball |

### Out of Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Defensive Line | Much Higher | Compress the space, force errors |
| Line of Engagement | Much Higher | Start the press in the opposition third |
| Trigger Press | Much More Often | Maximum pressing intensity |
| Prevent Short GK Distribution | ✓ Enabled | Force the goalkeeper to kick long |
| Get Stuck In | ✓ Enabled | Win the ball, not just pressure |
| Offside Trap | — | Risky with high line; better to just have fast CBs |
| Defensive Width | Force Inside | Funnel play into your DMs |

## Player Roles Deep Dive

### Goalkeeper
**Sweeper Keeper (Attack)**
- Must have: Rushing Out (14+), Acceleration (12+), Passing (12+)
- Acts as a sweeper behind the high line
- Don't use a traditional GK — they'll stay on their line while balls go over their head

### Defense
**Both Full-Backs → Full-Back (Support)**
- Must have: Stamina (14+), Positioning (13+), Crossing (12+)
- Support duty means they join attacks but don't abandon their post
- **Avoid Wing-Back (Attack)** — they'll leave giant gaps behind them

**Both CBs → Ball-Playing Defender (Defend)**
- Must have: Pace (13+), Passing (13+), Positioning (14+)
- BPD is essential — your CBs will receive the ball under pressure and need to pass out
- Speed is necessary for the high line; slow CBs = disaster

### Midfield Pivot
**Left DM → Segundo Volante (Support)**
- Must have: Stamina (15+), Work Rate (15+), Off the Ball (13+)
- The engine. Box-to-box but from deeper, joining attacks late

**Right DM → Deep-Lying Playmaker (Defend)**
- Must have: Passing (15+), Vision (15+), Positioning (14+), Composure (14+)
- Your Pirlo. Sits deep, dictates tempo, sprays passes. DOES NOT press aggressively — holds position

### Attacking Midfield
**AMC → Attacking Midfielder (Attack) or Shadow Striker**
- Must have: Finishing (13+), Passing (13+), Off the Ball (14+), Composure (13+)
- The second line of press and your primary chance creator

**Left Wing → Inside Forward (Attack)**
- Must have: Finishing (13+), Dribbling (14+), Acceleration (14+)
- Cuts inside and becomes a second striker

**Right Wing → Winger (Support)**
- Must have: Crossing (14+), Pace (14+), Dribbling (13+)
- Provides width and service. Support duty means better defensive contribution

### Striker
**Pressing Forward (Attack)**
- Must have: Work Rate (15+), Aggression (14+), Stamina (14+), Finishing (13+)
- The first defender. His pressing triggers the whole system
- Do NOT use Advanced Forward here — they won't press enough
- Do NOT use Target Forward — too slow and static

## Match-Day Adjustments

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Conceding from through balls | Lower defensive line one notch. Your CBs are too slow. |
| Low possession % | Reduce tempo to Slightly Lower. You're rushing transitions. |
| Forwards not scoring | Add "Work Ball Into Box". Too many low-quality long shots. |
| Players exhausted by 65' | Reduce trigger press intensity to "More Often" (one notch down) |
| Getting countered on wings | Switch full-backs to Defend duty, wingers to Support |
| 0-0 at halftime vs weaker team | Raise mentality to Attacking, add "Pass Into Space" |
| Leading 2-0 with 20' left | Drop mentality to Positive, trigger press → Slightly More |

## Training for Gegenpress

Your training schedule MUST prioritize:
1. **Physical** sessions 3x/week — build and maintain Stamina/Work Rate
2. **Tactical** sessions 2x/week — your pressing shape needs to be drilled
3. **Match Practice** — training matches build tactical familiarity faster than any drill

In the Individual Training screen, set all outfield players' Additional Focus to **Quickness** — even 1-2 points of extra Acceleration makes a huge difference.

## When NOT to Use Gegenpress

Gegenpress fails spectacularly when:
- Your team has **below-average Work Rate and Stamina** (check Team Report → Comparison)
- You're playing **3 games in 7 days** (players won't recover)
- It's **raining heavily** (heavy pitch = more energy cost, more injuries)
- The opponent plays **5 at the back with quick strikers** (they'll bypass your press with long balls)

For those scenarios, check our [3-5-2 Catenaccio Guide](/tactics/3-5-2-catenaccio) or [3-5-2 Counter-Attack Guide](/tactics/3-5-2-counter-attack).
