## Why Sign Wonderkids by Role, Not by Name

Every wonderkid list tells you who the stars are. Few tell you *which role each one actually fits* — and that's where signings go wrong. An €80m technician is worthless in a wing-play system, and a 6'4 target man is wasted as an inside forward.

This guide is organized the way you actually build a squad: **role by role**. Each of the 13 roles in our [Roles Encyclopedia](/roles) gets its best young players, the recommended duty, the budget tier, and a one-line reason why the profile fits.

> **Reader's note:** Roles overlap with our [FM26 Wonderkids by Formation](/blog/fm26-wonderkids-by-formation) list — the same elite players appear in both, but the *recommended role* is what changes between a 4-2-3-1 gegenpress and a 4-4-2 wing-play setup. Pick by formation when you've chosen a system; pick by role when you know the position you need.

## FAQ

### What are the best FM26 wonderkids for each role?

Warren Zaïre-Emery (box-to-box), Pau Cubarsí (ball-playing defender), Arda Güler (advanced playmaker), Lamine Yamal (inside forward) and Endrick (advanced forward) are the elite picks. For value, Adam Wharton, Ayyoub Bouaddi, Vitor Roque and Evan Ferguson offer the same roles for mid-tier fees.

### Who is the best young goalkeeper in FM26?

Guillaume Restes is the best young sweeper keeper, with elite Reflexes and distribution for a high line. Lucas Chevalier is the best traditional young shot-stopper, and Bart Verbruggen is the best modern sweeper behind a high defensive line.

### Who is the best young target forward in FM26?

Benjamin Šeško is the best young target man — 6'5 with 17 Jumping Reach and Heading. Samu Omorodion is the cheaper alternative with genuine pace, and Evan Ferguson adds a link-play dimension to the role.
