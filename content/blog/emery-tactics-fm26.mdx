## Emery Tactics in FM26: The Philosophy

Unai Emery turned Aston Villa into a Champions League side with a system that looks old-fashioned and plays anything but: a **pressing 4-4-2** that suffocates opponents in the middle third, then attacks directly the moment the ball is won.

The core principles:

1. **Two banks of four** — the 4-4-2 is a compact, narrow pressing base, not a defensive shell
2. **Coordinated press triggers** — the front two force play wide, the midfield four then trap the ball
3. **Vertical directness** — quick forward passes, wide 1v1s, runners in behind
4. **The 4-2-2-2 in possession** — the wide midfielders push up and the shape becomes a front four

## The Emery Formation in FM26

```
              SK (Su)

FB(A)   CD(D)   CD(D)   FB(S)

W(S)    DM(D)   BBM(S)   IW(S)

       DLF(A)
       AF(A)
```

**In defence this is a classic 4-4-2; in attack it becomes a 4-2-2-2.** The wide midfielders step into the half-spaces, the full-backs provide the width, and the front two split into a deep link and a runner.

| Position | Role | Emery Logic |
|---|---|---|
| GK | **Sweeper Keeper (Support)** | Sits high behind the line, quick distribution |
| CDs | **CD(D) + CD(D)** | Defend the line, win the second balls from the press |
| Full-backs | **FB(A) + FB(S)** | The left-back bombs on while the right-back balances |
| Midfield | **DM(D) + BBM(S)** | The DM screens; the BBM presses and arrives in the box |
| Wide Midfield | **W(S) + IW(S)** | One stays wide for 1v1s, one tucks inside — the 4-2-2-2 shape |
| Strikers | **DLF(A) + AF(A)** | The link and the runner — the two-man front line |

## Team Instructions: The Emery Settings

### In Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Width | Fairly Wide | Stretch the pitch for wide 1v1s |
| Passing | Mixed | Direct but controlled — not route one, not tiki-taka |
| Tempo | Higher | Vertical urgency after the press is beaten |
| Play Out of Defence | ON | Build through the pivot when the press allows |
| Pass Into Space | ON | Release the AF in behind early |

### In Transition

| Instruction | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Counter | ON | The press wins the ball, the counter wins the game |
| Counter-Press | ON | A quick 5-second counter-press, then reset into the 4-4-2 |
| GK Distribution | Roll It Out | Short restart to beat the first press |

### Out of Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Line of Engagement | **Higher** | Press in the middle third — the 4-4-2's natural hunting ground |
| Defensive Line | Higher | Squeeze with the midfield — no space between the banks |
| Trigger Press | More Often | Trap triggers: force play wide, then close the trap |
| Prevent Short GK Dist | ON | Force long kicks and win the second ball |
| Defensive Width | Narrow | The two banks of four stay tight — compactness first |

## The Press Trap in a 4-4-2

The 4-4-2 press works differently from a front-three press. The front two curve their runs to force the opponent wide, and the midfield four *then* collapse on the ball. In FM26:

1. **Set Opposition Instructions** on the wide players — force them onto their weaker foot
2. **The front two press with triggers, not constantly** — they hold the shape until the pass into midfield
3. **The BBM joins the front line when pressing** — a 4-4-2 becomes a 4-2-4 the moment the press triggers
4. **If the opponent bypasses the press with long balls** — drop the line of engagement to Standard and win the second ball instead

## Key Player Requirements

1. **Stamina 16+ for the wide midfielders** — they run the flank both ways in a 4-4-2
2. **Work Rate 15+ for everyone in the middle eight** — the press is a team action
3. **Pace 14+ for the AF** — the runner in behind must time the early release
4. **Strength 14+ for the DLF** — holding up play while the midfield arrives
5. **Aerial Presence 14+ for the CBs** — you concede crosses; win them all

## Common Mistakes

### Pressing with both banks simultaneously
If all eight players chase the ball, one pass splits the whole team. The front two press first; the midfield four press *on trigger*. Use OIs and player instructions to coordinate the trap.

### Symmetrical full-backs
Emery always keeps one full-back back. Two attacking full-backs in a 4-4-2 leaves a yawning gap in behind the midfield four. Set one FB(A) and one FB(S).

### Wide midfielders drifting inside
If both wide players come inside, the 4-2-2-2 collapses into a congested middle and the full-backs are stranded. Keep one wide player hugging the touchline for the 1v1 — that's where Bailey wins games.

## When Emery's System Struggles

- **Against elite possession sides** — the 4-4-2 press can be played around by teams with three elite technicians in midfield. Drop to a Standard line of engagement and counter through the flanks.
- **Against direct long-ball teams** — a high line invites the ball over the top. Keep the higher line only when your CBs win the aerial duel consistently.
