## Why Set Pieces Win Matches in FM26

Set pieces in FM26 are not an afterthought — they're a primary scoring method. In the current match engine, **15-25% of all goals come from dead ball situations**. That means a well-optimized set piece routine can be worth **10-15 extra goals per season**.

The best set piece tactics in FM26 combine three elements:
1. **Routine design** — Where your players stand, move, and attack
2. **Personnel matching** — Putting the right attributes on the right delivery/attack roles
3. **Match engine awareness** — Knowing which routines the current ME favors

This guide covers every dead ball scenario with setups that have been tested across multiple saves and tactics.

---

## Corner Tactics: Near Post Is Still King

The FM26 match engine continues to heavily favor **near-post corner delivery**. Here's the optimal setup:

### Attacking Corners — The "Near Post Cluster" Routine

| Role | Player Attributes Needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Corner Taker | **Corners 14+**, Crossing 14+ | In-swinging delivery (left-footer from right side, vice versa) |
| Near Post Attacker | **Jumping 16+**, Heading 15+, Bravery 14+ | Your best aerial CB — this is your primary goal threat |
| Lurk Outside Area | **Long Shots 14+**, Composure 13+ | Secondary threat for cleared balls |
| Attack Far Post | Jumping 14+, Heading 13+ | Clean-up duty |
| Stay Back | Acceleration 14+, Pace 14+ | Counter-attack prevention |

**Routine instructions:**
- Aim: **Near Post**
- Delivery: **Mixed** (in-swinging produces best results but mix keeps AI guessing)
- Players in box: 4-5 (don't overcrowd — too many bodies trigger defensive crowding)

**Expected return:** 8-12 corner goals per season with a top-half Championship side.

### Defending Corners — Zonal + Man Marking Hybrid

The best defensive setup isn't pure zonal or pure man — it's a hybrid:

```
┌────────────────────────────┐
│     Goalkeeper (Default)    │
│                             │
│  Mark Near Post (FB/Winger) │
│  2x Mark Tall (your CBs)    │
│  Man Mark × 3               │
│  Zonal × 2 (6-yard box)     │
│  Edge of Area × 1           │
│  Stay Forward × 1 (Pace 17+)│
└────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Mark Tall Player**: Assign to opposition CBs (they're the primary corner threats)
- **Zonal 6-yard box**: Two players covering the danger zone
- **Stay Forward**: Your fastest attacker — keeps AI from committing all defenders

## Free Kick Tactics: Direct vs Indirect

### Direct Free Kicks (Shooting Range: 18-25 yards)

In FM26, direct free kicks are the most improved area of the match engine. The best routine:

| Position | Setup |
|---|---|
| Taker | **Free Kicks 16+**, Long Shots 14+, Technique 14+ |
| Run over ball | Distracts wall, adds crossing threat |
| Stand with taker | Short option to confuse wall |
| Attack near/center/far | Standard positioning |

**Key finding in FM26 ME:** Free kicks aimed at the **goalkeeper's side** (the side they're not covering) convert at significantly higher rates than "over the wall" in previous versions. Train your taker's **"Tries Long Range Free Kicks"** PPM.

Expected return: 4-6 direct free kick goals per season with a specialist.

### Indirect Free Kicks (Wide Areas — Best Crossing Position)

For wide free kicks, FM26 heavily rewards the **"Best Header" routine**:

1. Delivery: **Cross — Aim at Best Header**
2. Assign your CB with Jumping 16+ as "Attack Near Post"  
3. Edge of area players for rebounds
4. Stay back × 2, Stay forward × 1

This is particularly effective because **wide indirect FKs in FM26 bypass offside traps** more effectively than in previous match engines.

### Indirect Free Kicks (Central, Deep)

Deep central free kicks should almost always go **short**. The long ball into the box has a conversion rate under 3%:

1. Pass short to midfield
2. Cycle possession
3. Look for the crossing opportunity from open play

## Long Throw Tactics: The Rory Delap Routine

Long throws in FM26 are **the most underrated set piece tool**. With a specialist (Long Throws 16+), you can create a corner-like scenario from the touchline.

### Setup for Long Flat Bullet Throw

| Role | Player | Key Attribute |
|---|---|---|
| Taker | Fullback/Midfielder | **Long Throws 16+** (flat bullet unlocks at 16) |
| Near Post Flick-On | Tall Striker | **Heading 15+**, Jumping 15+ |
| Attack Ball | Your best aerial CB | Jumping 16+, Heading 15+ |
| Lurk | Midfielder | Long Shots 14+ |

**Routine:** Aim at **Near Post** → Flick-on to **Center** → Attack Ball

This is essentially a corner routine but more unpredictable because AI defensive setups for long throws are weaker.

### How to Find a Long Throw Specialist

Use this player search filter:
- Long Throws ≥ 16
- Throw Ins attribute is cosmetic in FM26; **Long Throws is the only attribute that matters**
- Fullbacks and defensive midfielders are most likely to have it
- Lower-league wonder: Look in Scandinavian leagues, Irish League, and English League Two

## Penalties: Attributes Over Ratings

FM26's penalty system weights **attributes, not reputation**. The best penalty taker isn't your star striker if he has:

- **Composure 17+** — The single most important penalty attribute
- **Finishing 14+** or **Penalty Taking 14+**
- **"Places Shots"** PPM — Players with this trait convert ~12% more penalties

**Taker order:** 1) Composure 17+ with Places Shots PPM > 2) Pure Penalty Taking 16+ > 3) Composed midfielder > 4) Everyone else

## FM26 Set Piece Meta Summary

The best set piece tactics in FM26 boil down to:

1. **Near post corners with in-swinging delivery** → Your CB should be top scorer from dead balls
2. **Direct free kicks aimed at keeper's side** → The wall setup changed, exploit it
3. **Wide indirect FK crosses to Best Header** → Bypasses offside better than FM25
4. **Long Flat Bullet Throw → Near Post → Flick On** → Most underrated routine
5. **Composure over Finishing for penalties** → Train "Places Shots" PPM on your taker

Set pieces are the difference between 6th and 1st in a tight league. Every routine point counts.
