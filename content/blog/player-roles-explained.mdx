## Why Player Roles Are the Most Important Part of Your Tactic

Your formation tells players WHERE to stand. Player roles tell them WHAT to do. Most FM26 beginners spend hours tweaking team instructions while completely ignoring the most impactful tactical decision: role selection.

**A 4-2-3-1 with correct roles beats a 4-3-3 with random roles every time.**

In FM26, each player role comes with a set of "hard-coded" behaviors — the player instructions you can't change. Understanding these is the difference between a coherent tactic and 11 individuals running in different directions.

FM26 also reshuffled the role list itself. Several FM24 classics were renamed, a few disappeared entirely, and a batch of new roles arrived — the wide players now split into keep-width and track-back variants, centre-backs split into stop and cover, and there are new "outlet" runners built for counter-attacks. That means a lot of older guides (and muscle memory) will send you hunting for roles that no longer exist in game.

Everything below is the current list from our [Player Roles Encyclopedia](/roles) — all 72 roles, each linking to its own deep-dive page.

## The Role-Duty Framework

Every outfield role in FM26 is built on two components:

1. **Role** — The behavioral template. Determines movement patterns, passing decisions, defensive positioning.
2. **Duty** — The risk appetite. Defend (stay back), Support (balanced), Attack (push forward).

### The Three Duty Categories

| Duty | Mental Focus | Typical Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Defend** | Discipline, Positioning, Marking | Players who MUST stay back: CBs, holding mids |
| **Support** | Balance, Link-up, Transition | Players who connect defense to attack |
| **Attack** | Aggression, Finishing, Dribbling | Players who MUST create or score |

> **Duty availability is role-specific.** Not every role offers all three duties: the [Box-to-Box Midfielder](/roles/box-to-box-midfielder) is Support only, the [Poacher](/roles/poacher) and [Free Role](/roles/free-role) are Attack only, and the [Holding Full-Back](/roles/holding-full-back) is Defend only. Pick the role for the behavior, then check which duties it actually allows.

## Essential Role Combinations

### The Midfield Triangle (Most Important)

Every successful FM26 tactic has a functional midfield triangle:

```
         Runner (Support)     Creator (Attack)
                 \               /
                  Holder (Defend)
```

| Layer | Roles that fill it | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Holder** | [Defensive Midfielder](/roles/defensive-midfielder) (D), [Screening Defensive Midfielder](/roles/screening-defensive-midfielder) (D), [Deep-Lying Playmaker](/roles/deep-lying-playmaker) (D) | Screens defense, stays deep always |
| **Runner** | [Box-to-Box Midfielder](/roles/box-to-box-midfielder) (Su), [Wide Covering Central Midfielder](/roles/wide-covering-central-midfielder) (Su), [Box-to-Box Playmaker](/roles/box-to-box-playmaker) (Su) | High energy, connects both ends |
| **Creator** | [Advanced Playmaker](/roles/advanced-playmaker) (A), [Channel Midfielder](/roles/channel-midfielder) (A), [Midfield Playmaker](/roles/midfield-playmaker) (Su) | Unlocks defenses with passes and late runs |

**The golden rule:** If you have two runners and no holder, you concede counter-attacks. Two creators and no runner, and your attacks lack dynamism. One of each is the magic formula.

## Key Roles Explained (By Category)

### Goalkeepers (5 roles)

| Role | Best Duty | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| [Sweeper Keeper](/roles/sweeper-keeper) | Support | High defensive line tactics (gegenpress) |
| [Ball-Playing Goalkeeper](/roles/ball-playing-keeper) | Support | Possession systems — splits the press with his distribution |
| [Goalkeeper](/roles/goalkeeper) | Defend | Standard, lower risk |
| [Line-Holding Keeper](/roles/line-holding-keeper) | Defend | New in FM26 — sits deep behind a low block |
| [No-Nonsense Goalkeeper](/roles/no-nonsense-keeper) | Defend | New in FM26 — clears everything long, never gambles |

> [Sweeper Keeper](/roles/sweeper-keeper) on Attack is the meta for high-line tactics. Without it, through balls over your defense become undefendable.

### Centre-Backs (10 roles)

| Role | Best Duty | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| [Ball-Playing Defender](/roles/ball-playing-defender) | Defend | Playing out from the back, possession tactics |
| [Centre-Back](/roles/central-defender) | Defend | Safe, all-round defending in any shape |
| [Stopping Centre-Back](/roles/stopping-centre-back) | Defend | Steps out early to meet attackers head-on |
| [Covering Centre-Back](/roles/covering-centre-back) | Defend | Drops behind the line to sweep up danger |
| [No-Nonsense Centre-Back](/roles/no-nonsense-centre-back) | Defend | Wins it, clears it — lower leagues, deep blocks |
| [Advanced Centre-Back](/roles/advanced-centre-back) | Support | FM26's modern libero — carries the ball into midfield |
| [Wide Centre-Back](/roles/wide-centre-back) | Defend | Back threes — holds width, engages wide runners |
| [Covering Wide Centre-Back](/roles/covering-wide-centre-back) / [Stopping Wide Centre-Back](/roles/stopping-wide-centre-back) | Defend | The same cover/stop split for the outer pair in a back three |
| [Overlapping Centre-Back](/roles/overlapping-centre-back) | Support | Back three — steps out and overlaps on the flank as an extra body |

**Syncing CB roles**: FM26 split the old centre-back job into stopper and coverer. Pair a [Stopping Centre-Back](/roles/stopping-centre-back) with a [Covering Centre-Back](/roles/covering-centre-back) and one of them is always in the right place: the stopper engages, the coverer sweeps in behind. In a back three, use the [Wide Centre-Back](/roles/wide-centre-back) roles for the outer pair so they can hold width.

| Advanced Playmaker / DLP hybrid | [Midfield Playmaker](/roles/midfield-playmaker) | One controller for both jobs |
| Mezzala | [Channel Midfielder](/roles/channel-midfielder) | **No direct replacement** — closest fit: attacks the half-space |
| Segundo Volante | [Box-to-Box Midfielder](/roles/box-to-box-midfielder) | **No direct replacement** — closest fit: late runs from the pivot |

## Common Role Mistakes

1. **Too many Attack duties** — Max 3-4 Attack duties in a tactic. More than that and your defensive shape collapses.
2. **No holder in midfield** — Every tactic needs at least one midfielder who always stays back: a [Screening Defensive Midfielder](/roles/screening-defensive-midfielder) or a [Deep-Lying Playmaker](/roles/deep-lying-playmaker) on Defend.
3. **Inside Forward + Wing-Back (Attack) on the same side** — They run into the same space. Use IF(At) + FB(Su) or W(Su) + WB(At) instead.
4. **Lone striker as Poacher** — A lone poacher is useless. They need a partner, or use a [Centre Forward](/roles/centre-forward), [Deep-Lying Forward](/roles/deep-lying-forward) or [Tracking Centre Forward](/roles/tracking-centre-forward).
5. **Playmaker overload** — Two playmakers attract the ball to the same area. Rarely effective (DLP + AP is fine if positioned far apart).
6. **Two stoppers at centre-back** — FM26's [Stopping Centre-Back](/roles/stopping-centre-back) steps out of the line. Pair one with a [Covering Centre-Back](/roles/covering-centre-back), never with another stopper.
7. **Copying FM24 role names straight across** — Half the list was renamed or removed. Check the old role names table above before you rebuild an old tactic.
