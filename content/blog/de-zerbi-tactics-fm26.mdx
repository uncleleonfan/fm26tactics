## De Zerbi Tactics in FM26: The Philosophy

Roberto De Zerbi's tactical approach isn't about a formation — it's about a **build-up philosophy**. The key principles:

1. **Draw the press deep** — Invite pressure in your own third to create space higher up
2. **Central overloads** — Pack the middle to progress through the thirds
3. **Third-man runs** — Use decoy movement to free the next pass
4. **Striker drops deep** — Creates a 4v3 or 5v4 in midfield build-up

---

## The De Zerbi Formation in FM26

```
             SK (Support)
        
    BPD(D)  CD(D)   BPD(D)

WB(S)   DLP(S)  BBM(S)   WB(S)

      IF(A)          IW(S)

            DLF(A)
```

**This is effectively a 4-2-3-1 that morphs into a 4-2-4 in possession.** Here's why each role matters:

| Position | Role | De Zerbi Logic |
|---|---|---|
| GK | **Sweeper Keeper (Support)** | Short distribution, plays as 11th outfield player |
| CDs | **Ball-Playing Defenders** | Crucial for drawing the press — must have Composure 14+ |
| Fullbacks | **Wing-Backs (Support)** | Stay wide in buildup, then underlap in final third |
| DM Double-Pivot | **DLP(S) + BBM(S)** | The DLP dictates tempo; BBM makes third-man runs |
| Wide Forwards | **IF(A) left, IW(S) right** | Asymmetric — IF cuts inside, IW stays wide for switches |
| Striker | **Deep-Lying Forward (Attack)** | Drops into midfield to create overload, then spins in behind |

## Team Instructions: The De Zerbi Build-Up

### In Possession — The Most Important Section

```
Attacking Width: FAIRLY NARROW
Approach Play: PLAY OUT OF DEFENCE
Passing Directness: SHORT PASSING
Tempo: SLIGHTLY LOWER
Time Wasting: NEVER
Play For Set Pieces: ✅ (for restarts and tactical resets)

Overlap/Underlap: UNDERLAP BOTH SIDES
Focus Play: THROUGH THE MIDDLE
Dribbling: RUN AT DEFENCE
```

**Why narrow + underlap:** De Zerbi's teams overload the center to bait the press, then release the wing-backs into the vacated wide spaces. The narrow attacking width forces opponents to compact centrally — exactly where the overload is.

### In Transition

```
When possession is lost: COUNTER-PRESS
When possession is won: HOLD SHAPE
Goalkeeper: ROLL IT OUT → CENTRE-BACKS
```

**Key detail:** "Hold Shape" on winning possession is deliberate. De Zerbi doesn't want chaotic counters — he wants to restart the controlled build-up from the back.

### Out of Possession

```
Trigger Press: MUCH MORE OFTEN
Prevent Short GK Distribution: ✅
Defensive Line: HIGHER
Press Line: MUCH HIGHER
Trigger Press: Tight Marking off ✅ (maintain shape)
Tackling: STAY ON FEET
```

The high line is non-negotiable. De Zerbi's Brighton played with the highest defensive line in the Premier League. In FM26, you need CBs with **Pace 14+** and **Anticipation 15+** to make this work.

## Player Requirements for De Zerbi FM26

### The Goalkeeper — Your Deepest Playmaker

```
First Touch 14+ | Composure 15+ | Passing 13+ | Kicking 14+
```
The sweeper keeper needs to receive under pressure and find the CBs. Don't use a traditional shot-stopper.

### Centre-Backs — Press Bait

```
Composure 16+ | First Touch 13+ | Passing 14+ | Pace 14+
```
These are the most important players in the system. They'll receive the ball with a striker closing down — they need to stay calm and find the pass.

### Wing-Backs — The Release Valve

```
Work Rate 15+ | Crossing 13+ | Off The Ball 14+ | Stamina 15+
```
They run the entire flank. In FM26, you'll need a backup for each because their condition will drop to 65-70% by minute 70.

### Double Pivot — The Engine Room

**DLP (Support):** Passing 16+, Vision 15+, Decisions 15+, Composure 15+. This is your Mac Allister/Gross — the one who dictates everything.

**BBM (Support):** Work Rate 16+, Stamina 16+, Off The Ball 14+, Long Shots 13+. This is your Caicedo — the destroyer who also arrives late in the box.

## FM26 Match Engine Specifics

After extensive testing in FM26 ME, here are critical adjustments that differ from pure De Zerbi theory:

### 1. Sweeper Keeper Distribution

De Zerbi wants the GK to play short to CBs every time. In FM26, set:
- **Distribute to Centre-Backs**
- **Take Short Kicks** (not Roll It Out — short kicks in FM26 are more reliable for beating a high press)

### 2. The Press Trap

In real life, De Zerbi baits the press then plays through. In FM26, this requires a specific setup:
- **Press Much More Often** + **Higher Line** — this is aggressive but necessary
- If you're conceding too many through balls, drop to **Higher Press** (not Much More) and keep **Higher Line**

### 3. Attacking Width Adjustment

Against teams that sit back (low block), temporarily widen to **Fairly Wide + Hit Early Crosses**. The narrow overload stops working when the opponent refuses to press.

## Results You Can Expect

Based on test saves with mid-table Premier League sides:

- **Possession:** 58-65% (league-leading)
- **Pass completion:** 88-92%  
- **xG per game:** 1.8-2.3
- **Goals from open play build-up:** ~70%
- **Weakness:** Vulnerable to 5-3-2 counter systems that bypass the press with long balls

The De Zerbi approach in FM26 is not about winning every game — it's about dominating the ball and creating high-quality chances. When it works, it produces some of the most beautiful football in the match engine.
