The most common reason a copied tactic fails is not the tactic. It is the club.

Every system on this site asks for a specific squad profile. A gegenpress without stamina is a system that stops working after an hour. A high-line possession system without quick centre-backs is a system that concedes breakaways. The right club makes an average system look clever; the wrong one makes a good system look broken.

This guide matches all ten coach systems in our library to the clubs that can play them without a rebuild. They are grouped by what they ask of the squad, because that is the decision you are actually making.

If you have not settled on a system yet, the two comparison articles do that job: [five control systems compared](/blog/fm26-positional-control-managers) and the [pragmatic low-block systems compared](/blog/fm26-low-block-counter-managers).

## Control systems

These systems want the ball, and the price is paid at the back. The squad question is simple: can your defenders play, or can they run? Very few FM26 squads can do both.

### Guardiola's 3-2-5

**The need:** First touch and passing throughout the front five, two controllers who can receive under pressure, defenders with the Positioning to play in midfield.

| Tier | Clubs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elite | **Man City**, **Bayern**, **Barcelona** | The only squads built for positional play from day one |
| Great fit | **Arsenal**, **Real Madrid** | One or two signings from perfect |
| Overachiever | **Ajax**, **Benfica** | Elite youth production gets there in two or three seasons |

**Best pick:** Man City. Full breakdown in [Guardiola's 3-2-5](/blog/guardiola-tactics-fm26).

### De Zerbi's build-up

**The need:** Centre-backs who can receive under pressure, a pivot comfortable with his back to goal, wing-backs with 16+ stamina.

| Tier | Clubs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elite | **Man City**, **Barcelona**, **Bayern** | Technical centre-backs plus relentless work rate |
| Great fit | **Brighton**, **Napoli**, **Real Sociedad** | The philosophy's natural homes |
| Overachiever | **Marseille**, **Borussia Mönchengladbach** | Good technical bases, room to build |

**Best pick:** Brighton. Full breakdown in the [De Zerbi build-up machine](/blog/de-zerbi-tactics-fm26).

### Arteta, Alonso, Nagelsmann, Flick and Emery

These five are covered together because the squad question splits them cleanly:

| System | Best fits | The deciding attribute |
|---|---|---|
| Arteta's 2-3-5 | Arsenal, Man City, Bayern | A positionally elite inverted full-back |
| Alonso's 3-4-2-1 | Leverkusen, Inter, Atalanta | Wing-backs with 16+ stamina and two roaming 10s |
| Nagelsmann's 4-2-3-1 | Bayern, Leipzig, Dortmund | Pace and Decisions at centre-back |
| Flick's high line | Barcelona, Bayern, Ajax | The fastest centre-backs you can find |
| Emery's 4-4-2 | Aston Villa, Villarreal, Sevilla | Wide midfielders who run both ways |

Each one, with the full role setup and the failure modes, is in [FM26 control managers compared](/blog/fm26-positional-control-managers).

## Pressing systems

Pressing asks for athletes rather than technicians, which makes it the friendliest school for mid-table saves and the least forgiving for squads in decline.

### Klopp's gegenpress

**The need:** Work Rate and Stamina 16+ across midfield, Accelerations 15+ up front, and two players for every pressing position.

| Tier | Clubs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elite | **Liverpool**, **RB Leipzig**, **Dortmund** | Pressing-ready squads from day one |
| Great fit | **Leverkusen**, **Brighton** | Young, hard-working, one or two signings from elite |
| Overachiever | **VfB Stuttgart**, **Crystal Palace** | Cheap pressing talent, big first-season upside |

**Best pick:** RB Leipzig — the youngest gegenpress squad in FM26. Full breakdown in [Klopp's gegenpress](/blog/klopp-tactics-fm26), with the practical build in the [step-by-step gegenpress setup](/blog/gegenpress-setup-guide).

## Pragmatic systems

The three systems that give up the ball on purpose. They need the most specific player type of any school, and they are the most reliable route to overachieving with a weak squad.

### Mourinho's low block

**The need:** Three centre-backs who win everything in the air, wing-backs who defend first, two direct strikers who can turn defence into attack in two passes.

| Tier | Clubs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elite | **Roma**, **Inter**, **Atlético** | The block-and-counter profile already exists |
| Great fit | **Juventus**, **Lazio** | Solid defensive bases, one or two signings away |
| Overachiever | **Bologna**, **Getafe** | The system's biggest fans — built for small budgets |

**Best pick:** Roma.

### Simeone's aggressive 4-4-2

**The need:** Aggression and Bravery across the middle, physical centre-backs, wide midfielders who track for 90 minutes.

| Tier | Clubs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elite | **Atlético Madrid** | The squad is the tactic |
| Great fit | **Boca Juniors**, **River Plate** | Argentinian intensity fits the profile exactly |
| Overachiever | **Celta Vigo**, **Espanyol** | Physical, aggressive, punch above their weight |

**Best pick:** Atlético Madrid — no other club fits one system this precisely.

### Ancelotti's compact 4-3-3

**The need:** Three fast forwards, one elite passer in the pivot, a channel midfielder who arrives in the box, and defenders who stay disciplined rather than aggressive.

| Tier | Clubs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elite | **Real Madrid**, **Napoli** | Vertical squads with elite individual quality |
| Great fit | **AC Milan**, **Aston Villa** | Strong midfields, fast forwards, room to grow |
| Overachiever | **Sevilla**, **Marseille** | Physical squads that overperform on the break |

**Best pick:** Real Madrid — the system was built for that exact squad.

All three, side by side with their instruction sets, are in the [low block and counter systems compared](/blog/fm26-low-block-counter-managers).

## How to choose your save

**Want to win immediately?** Pick the Elite club that matches your favourite system — Man City for control, Liverpool for pressing, Real Madrid for the pragmatic game.

**Want a medium-term project?** Take a Great fit club and add two players. Every system here has a club in that tier that is one window from being ideal.

**Want the hardest challenge?** Take a defensive system to an overachiever club in a league where the top sides are much stronger. The low block and counter systems overachieve more with small budgets than any pressing system does.

**Want to test two systems?** Pick a club that appears in two tiers above — Leverkusen and Aston Villa both work in more than one school, which makes them good save choices if you want to change tactic mid-project without changing club.

## Building the tactic itself

Once you have the club, the mechanics are the same everywhere: pick the base formation, assign the eleven roles, then set the instructions in possession, transition and out of possession — in that order, because changing transitions first is how tactics end up unstable.
