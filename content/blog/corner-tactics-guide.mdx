## Set Pieces: The Hidden Goal Factory

In a typical FM26 season, **30-40% of all goals come from set pieces.** That includes corners, free kicks, throw-ins, and penalties. Corners alone account for 10-15% of total goals — roughly **8-12 goals per season** for an average team.

For perspective: that's the difference between finishing 6th and 4th. Between Europa League and Champions League. Between survival and relegation. And yet 90% of FM players leave their corner routines on default.

---

## The Meta: Near Post (Patch 26.1+)

In the current FM26 match engine, **near-post corners** are the most consistent source of corner goals. The setup is simple and reliable.

### Step 1: Set Your Taker

In Tactic → Set Pieces → Corners, assign:
- **Left corner**: Best left-footed taker (Crossing 14+, Corners 13+)
- **Right corner**: Best right-footed taker

**Inswinging corners produce more goals than outswingers** in the FM26 engine. If you can, use a left-footer for right-side corners and vice versa. The ball curves towards goal, making it harder for keepers to claim.

### Step 2: Set the Routine (Near Post)

```
Corner Aim: Near Post

Player Positioning (Left Corner):
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ • Best Header (CB)    → Attack Near Post │
│ • Second Best Header  → Attack Far Post  │
│ • Best Finisher       → Attack from Edge │
│ • DM/BWM              → Stay Back         │
│ • AM/Winger (Left)    → Offer Short      │
│ • AM/Winger (Right)   → Lurk Outside     │
│ • Remaining           → Go Forward        │
│ • Both Full-Backs     → Stay Back         │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Key attributes for your Near Post attacker:**
- Jumping Reach: 16+
- Heading: 15+
- Bravery: 14+
- Strength: 14+
- Anticipation: 13+

A 6'5" center-back with 17 Jumping Reach attacking the near post is borderline unstoppable. Look for Virgil van Dijk, Nikola Milenkovic, or Perr Schuurs types.

### Step 3: Train for It

Schedule **"Chance Conversion - Near Post Flick On"** set piece training weekly. Even 3-4 sessions increase your near-post conversion rate by ~15%.

## Far Post Routine (Alternative)

If your best header is 6'3"+ but slow (low Acceleration), use Far Post instead. Tall players win headers at the far post by simply being bigger — speed matters less.

**Same positioning as Near Post, but:**
- Corner Aim: Far Post
- Your best header attacks Far Post
- Second header attacks Near Post (for knock-downs)

The far-post routine also works well against teams with a short goalkeeper. Check the scout report before the match — if the opponent's GK is under 6'0", far-post crosses are very dangerous.

---

## Short Corner Routine (Control/Possession Play Style)

If you're playing Tiki-Taka or Control Possession and don't want to risk a counter-attack from a cleared corner:

1. Set corner aim to **Short**
2. Two players → Offer Short
3. Taker passes short → short corner taker passes back → taker crosses from a better angle
4. This also pulls defenders out of position, creating chaos in the box

Short corners work best when you have a technically gifted midfielder (Passing 16+, Technique 15+, Vision 15+) to orchestrate the short play.

## Defending Corners (Equally Important)

You concede from corners too. The default setup is actually decent, but optimize it:

```
Corner Defending:
├── 2 Best Headers  → Mark Tall Player
├── 3 Average       → Man Mark
├── Best Anticipator → Mark Near Post (this is THE guy)
├── Fastest Player  → Stay Forward (counter-attack outlet)
├── 2 Full-Backs    → Mark Far Post / Edge of Area
├── Remaining       → Go Back
```

**Mark Near Post** is the most important defensive corner instruction. A player with Anticipation 15+, Positioning 14+, and Heading 13+ stationed at the near post blocks ~40% of near-post corner goals — the most common type.

**Stay Forward** keeps one fast player up. Clear the ball to him and you'll get 3-4 easy counter-attack goals per season. Choose your quickest player with decent First Touch (12+).

## Penalty Area Free Kicks

Similar to corners but with different targeting:

| FK Position | Aim | Best Tactic |
|---|---|---|
| Central (18-25 yards) | Over wall | FK attribute 15+ taker |
| Wide (near corner flag) | Near Post | Treat exactly like a corner |
| Deep (30+ yards) | Far Post | Best header + cross |
