## Arteta Tactics in FM26: The Philosophy

Mikel Arteta turned Arsenal into title contenders with a system built on **structured control**. Unlike De Zerbi's bait-and-press approach, Arteta's teams don't invite pressure — they *remove* it by occupying every passing lane before the opposition can press.

The core principles:

1. **The 2-3-5 shape** — the left-back inverts into midfield, creating a three-man build-up behind a five-man front line
2. **Half-space domination** — the number 8s receive between the lines, never on the touchline
3. **Rest defence** — a 3-2 structure that survives losing the ball instantly
4. **Set pieces as a weapon** — Arsenal scored a league-leading 13 set-piece goals in 2024-25; this is a feature, not a bonus

## The Arteta Formation in FM26

```
              SK (Su)

FB(A)   BPD(D)   CD(D)   IFB(D)

       DLP(D)   BBM(S)

IF(A)          IW(S)
       AM(A)
       DLF(A)
```

**Read that again: the left-back is inverted.** In possession this becomes a 2-3-5: two centre-backs, a midfield three of IFB + DLP + BBM, and a front five of FB(A), both wide forwards, the AM, and the striker.

| Position | Role | Arteta Logic |
|---|---|---|
| GK | **Sweeper Keeper (Support)** | First line of the build-up, comfortable receiving under pressure |
| CDs | **BPD(D) + CD(D)** | The BPD switches play; the CD holds the line |
| Left-back | **Inverted Full-Back (Defend)** | Tucks into midfield — the third man in the build-up |
| Right-back | **Full-Back (Attack)** | Provides the width the inverted left-back gives up |
| Midfield | **DLP(D) + BBM(S)** | The DLP dictates from deep; the BBM arrives late |
| Attacking Mid | **Advanced Playmaker (Attack)** | The Odegaard role — receives in the right half-space |
| Wingers | **IF(A) + IW(S)** | Tuck inside to crowd the box and press the full-backs |
| Striker | **Deep-Lying Forward (Attack)** | Drops to link, then attacks the box late |

## Team Instructions: The Arteta Settings

### In Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Width | Fairly Narrow | Compact structure — the width comes from the right-back and switches |
| Passing | Shorter | Controlled progression, no risky hopeful balls |
| Tempo | Standard | Patient until the half-space opens, then accelerate |
| Play Out of Defence | ON | Non-negotiable — the 2-3-5 starts from the keeper |
| Work Ball Into Box | ON | Arsenal lead the league in low-xG shot prevention |
| Focus Play | Through the Middle | Attack the half-spaces, not the touchline |

### In Transition

| Instruction | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Counter | ON | Win it, move it forward quickly |
| Counter-Press | ON | Recover in 5 seconds, then fall into the 4-4-2 rest defence |
| GK Distribution | Roll It Out | Short restart to the centre-backs |

### Out of Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Line of Engagement | Higher | Press in the middle third, not the opponent's box |
| Defensive Line | Higher | Compact with the midfield — no gaps between lines |
| Trigger Press | More Often | Press triggers based on passes into the half-space |
| Prevent Short GK Dist | ON | Force long kicks, then win the second ball |
| Defensive Width | Narrow | Force play into the crowded middle |

## The Inverted Full-Back: The System's Engine

The IFB is the most important role in this system, and it needs specific attributes:

```
Positioning 15+ | Decisions 15+ | Passing 14+ | First Touch 14+ | Composure 14+
```

If the IFB has poor positioning, the space behind him (where the FB(A) should be covering) becomes a constant liability. If you don't have a player who fits — **do not force it**. Use a traditional Full-Back (Support) and adjust the DLP to a Half-Back instead; the shape changes but the control remains.

---

## Key Player Requirements

1. **First Touch & Composure 14+ for the back four** — they receive under immediate pressure
2. **Positioning 15+ for the IFB and DLP** — the rest defence depends on them
3. **Work Rate 16+ for the number 8s** — they cover both boxes in every match
4. **Off the Ball 14+ for the front five** — half-space movement is the entire attack
5. **Aerial Presence 14+ for CBs and the striker** — the set-piece threat needs targets

## Common Mistakes

### Both full-backs inverting
If *both* full-backs tuck inside, you have no width at all. Arteta always keeps one full-back wide — usually the one opposite the inverting side. In FM26, set one IFB and one attacking FB.

### Static number 8s
The 2-3-5 only works if the 8s *move*. An AM and 8 who stand still collapse the half-space game. Look for players with high Off the Ball and instruct them to Roam From Position.

### Treating set pieces as optional
Arteta's Arsenal won games from corners and free-kicks alone. Assign a dedicated set-piece coach, add routines, and target your best headers. In FM26 this is a 10-15 goal swing across a season.

---

## When Arteta's System Struggles

- **Against deep, narrow blocks** — if the opponent refuses to engage, the half-space game stalls. Add "Hit Early Crosses" and let the right-back become a crossing threat.
- **Against elite counter-attacks** — a narrow rest defence is vulnerable to quick wingers in the space behind the FB(A). Drop the right-back to Support in big away games.
