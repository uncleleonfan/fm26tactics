## Guardiola Tactics in FM26: The Philosophy

Pep Guardiola's Manchester City is the most **structured** system in modern football. Where Arteta removes the press and De Zerbi baits it, Guardiola *plans around it*: every player has a lane, every lane has a job, and the ball is moved through the geometry of the pitch.

The core principles:

1. **The 3-2-5 attacking shape** — from a 4-3-3 base, one centre-back steps up and one full-back inverts
2. **The box midfield** — four players occupy the middle in a 2-2 diamond, making the press impossible to cut through
3. **Positional play, not improvisation** — the system wins the space before the ball arrives
4. **Counter-press as a shield** — lose the ball high, win it back in 5 seconds

## The Guardiola Formation in FM26

```
              SK (Su)

IFB(D)  BPD(D)  CD(D)   FB(A)

       HB(D)   DLP(S)

IW(S)          SS(A) / AM(A)
       IF(A)          IW(S)
       DLF(A)
```

**Read the shape carefully: the right-back is an Inverted Full-Back and the left-back attacks.** In possession this becomes a 3-2-5 — the IFB joins the midfield two, and the left-back pushes into the front line.

| Position | Role | Guardiola Logic |
|---|---|---|
| GK | **Sweeper Keeper (Support)** | The 11th outfield player — City build from the keeper |
| Back three | **IFB(D) + BPD(D) + CD(D)** | The IFB steps into midfield; the BPD switches play |
| Left-back | **Full-Back (Attack)** | The width on the inverted side — the 5 in 3-2-5 |
| Midfield | **HB(D) + DLP(S)** | The Half-Back drops into the back three; the DLP dictates |
| Attacking Mid | **SS(A)** | The De Bruyne/Foden role — arrives in the box late |
| Wingers | **IF(A) + IW(S)** | Tuck inside to crowd the box and press the full-backs |
| Striker | **Deep-Lying Forward (Attack)** | The false-nine movement — drops to create the overload |

## Team Instructions: The Guardiola Settings

### In Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Width | Fairly Narrow | The box midfield lives in the middle — width comes from full-backs only |
| Passing | Shorter | Every pass is a positional move, not a gamble |

| Play Out of Defence | ON | Non-negotiable — the 3-2-5 starts from the keeper |

## The Box Midfield: Why the Press Can't Touch It

The 2-2 midfield box (HB + DLP behind, IW + SS ahead) is Guardiola's greatest invention. It gives every passing lane an option and every presser a decoy:

1. **Four passing options in the middle** — pressing one unlocks the other three
2. **The IFB makes it a 3-2** — the back line stays secure while the box attacks
3. **The SS and IW swap positions constantly** — the press can never lock onto a man
4. **In FM26, set both central players to Roam From Position** — static midfielders kill the box

The box only works with elite passing and off-the-ball movement. If your midfielders can't play one-touch, this system becomes sideways possession with no threat.

---

## Key Player Requirements

1. **First Touch 15+ for every outfield player** — one-touch play is the entire system
2. **Passing 15+ for the midfield box** — the box needs four passing lanes, not four runners
3. **Positioning 15+ for the IFB and HB** — the 3-2-5 collapses if the shapes are wrong
4. **Composure 15+ under pressure** — City play out against the highest presses
5. **Off the Ball 15+ for the front five** — positional play is movement, not sprinting

## Common Mistakes

### Inverting both full-backs
City always keep one full-back wide to complete the 5 in the 3-2-5. Two inverted full-backs leave you with no width and no front-line balance.

### Static box midfielders
The box is a *movement* structure. If the DLP and SS stand still, the box is just four players standing in the middle of the pitch. Use Roam From Position and look for elite off-the-ball movement.

### Playing it against weaker teams
This system dominates the ball but can underwhelm against deep blocks — the geometry creates passing lanes, not goal chances. Add the "Hit Early Crosses" outlet and a target in the box when opponents park the bus.

---

## When Guardiola's System Struggles

- **Against elite transition teams** — the 3-2-5 leaves three at the back against pacey front threes. In big away games, keep one full-back home and drop the SS to a 4-3-3.
- **Against physical mid-blocks** — a team that sits in a 5-4-1 and lets you have the ball needs patience, not system changes. Keep the shape, raise the tempo gradually, and wait for the half-space opening.
