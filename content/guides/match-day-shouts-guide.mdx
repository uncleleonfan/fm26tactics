## You're Not Just Watching — You're Managing

The biggest gap between good FM26 players and great ones isn't tactic design — it's what they do during the 90 minutes. Match day management turns draws into wins and losses into points.

This guide covers the three pillars of in-game management: **Shouts**, **Substitutions**, and **Tactical Tweaks**.

---

## Part 1: The Shout System (Returned in Update 26.1)

Shouts are short touchline instructions that modify your players' behavior for the next phase of play. They don't change your formation or roles — they adjust **mentality and focus**.

### Shout Basics
- **Cooldown**: 10 minutes between shouts
- **Activation**: Takes effect at the next dead ball (throw-in, free kick, goal kick)
- **Scope**: Affects the entire team equally

### The 8 Shouts — When and Why

| Shout | When to Use | Context |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Encourage** | Team is struggling, heads are dropping | Against stronger opposition, after conceding, playing with 10 men |
| **Calm Down** | Team is reckless, cards piling up | Rivalry matches, high-aggression players losing composure |
| **Focus** | Leading but looking complacent | Up 1-0, late in the match, easy opposition |
| **Fire Up** | Dial things back in a big moment | Prevent over-excitement in crucial phases |
| **No Pressure** | Expected to lose | Cup match vs giant, relegation battle away |
| **Demand More** | Losing and not responding | Down 0-1, performance is flat, no urgency |
| **Praise** | Everything is going well | 2-0 up, playing beautifully |
| **Berate** | Embarrassingly bad performance | Losing badly to an inferior team, unacceptable effort |

### The Shout Cadence
- **0-15 min**: Observe. Don't shout immediately.
- **15-25 min**: If struggling → Encourage or Demand More
- **30-45 min**: If leading → Focus (prevent complacency before halftime)
- **Half-time team talk**: Separate from shouts — use this for bigger morale shifts
- **45-60 min**: Observe. Let the team talk settle.
- **60-75 min**: Key window. Leading → Focus + Calm Down. Losing → Demand More.
- **75-90 min**: One final shout — Praise or Demand More depending on situation

> **Critical**: Don't spam shouts every 10 minutes. Players get "confused by the feedback" if you over-communicate. 2-3 well-timed shouts per half is optimal.

## Part 2: Substitution Strategy

Substitutions in FM26 aren't just about replacing tired legs — they're tactical weapons.

### The Fatigue Rule
Check **Player → Body Language** or the fitness icon. Sub when:
- Condition drops below **65%** for high-intensity roles (B2B, WB, Pressing Forward)
- Condition drops below **60%** for standard roles
- Any player shows "Tired" or "Exhausted" — sub immediately

### The Mental State Rule
Check body language:
- **Nervous** / **Anxious** → Sub or shout "No Pressure"
- **Complacent** → Shout "Focus" first, sub if it doesn't change
- **Aggressive** → Shout "Calm Down" first, sub if on a yellow card
- **Frustrated** → Sub — this player is about to make a mistake

### The Card Rule
- Player on a **yellow card** with 30+ minutes remaining → consider subbing, especially if:
  - They're a defender facing a pacy winger
  - They're a midfielder in a physical battle
  - The ref has high cards-per-game average (check pre-match)

### Optimal Sub Timing
- **First sub**: 55-65 minutes — catch fatigue before it becomes a problem
- **Second sub**: 65-75 minutes — tactical adjustment or second fatigue sub
- **Third sub**: 75-85 minutes — see out the game, or throw on an attacker
- Never make all 5 subs at once — stagger them to maintain rhythm

### Substitution Types
| Type | When | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| **Fatigue sub** | Like-for-like, fresh legs | Sub off B2B for another B2B at 60' |
| **Tactical sub** | Change shape or approach | Sub a second striker for an extra midfielder |
| **Card sub** | Protect a booked player | Sub yellow-carded DM at 55' |
| **Impact sub** | Throw on game-changers | Bring on a pace merchant at 70' against tired legs |
| **Time-wasting sub** | Kill clock in stoppage time | Any defensive sub in the 88th minute |

## Part 3: Reading the Match Engine in Real Time

Don't just watch highlights — read the match stats:

### What to Check at 30 Minutes
- **Possession** — Are you controlling the game or being dominated?
- **Pass completion** — Below 80%? Your tempo might be too high.
- **Shots / On Target** — Shots from 25 yards out? Add "Work Ball Into Box."

### What to Check at 60 Minutes
- **Where are shots coming from?** — Opposition shots from the same area repeatedly → mark that zone tighter
- **Which flank is weak?** — If all crosses come from one side → adjust full-back duty or midfield cover
- **Player ratings** — Anyone below 6.4? They're having a bad game. Consider subbing.

### The "One Change at a Time" Principle
When you identify a problem, change **one thing** and watch for 10-15 minutes before changing another. Changing mentality + formation + three roles simultaneously makes it impossible to know what worked.

## Part 4: Common Match Scenarios

### Scenario: You're 1-0 Up at 60 Minutes
1. Shout: **Focus**
2. Check body language — any Complacent players?
3. Sub #1: Fatigue — replace your most demanding role
4. 70 min: Consider shifting mentality from Positive → Balanced
5. 80 min: Add "Waste Time" instruction, Slow Pace Down
6. 85 min: Third sub (time-wasting + defensive cover)

### Scenario: You're 0-1 Down at 60 Minutes
1. Shout: **Demand More**
2. Check stats: Where are you failing to create?
3. If lacking penetration → Increase tempo, add "Pass Into Space"
4. If lacking creativity → Change one midfielder to a more attacking role
5. Sub #1 (60'): Impact sub — pacey attacker
6. Sub #2 (70'): Change shape if needed (e.g. 4-3-3 → 4-2-4)
7. 80 min: Mentality → Attacking, final impact sub

### Scenario: Opponent is Dominating (0-0 or Trailing)
1. Shout: **Calm Down** — your team is likely panicking and losing shape
2. Check: Are they playing through your midfield? → Drop defensive line 1 notch
3. If being overrun in midfield → sub a second DM or change a BBM to BWM
4. Shout: **No Pressure** — takes the pressure off and can reset mentality
5. Don't chase the game too early — keep it tight until 70th minute, then open up
6. If still under siege at 75' → 5-3-2 or 5-4-1 formation change, park the bus

### Scenario: Last 10 Minutes — Need a Goal (0-1 Down)
1. Shout: **Demand More** — urgency must go up
2. Mentality: **Attacking** or **Very Attacking**
3. Add "Pass Into Space" and "Work Ball Into Box" team instructions
4. Sub: throw on your fastest striker or most creative player
5. Formation: push a midfielder up — 4-3-3 → 4-2-4 or 3-5-2 → 3-4-3
6. Shout: **Fire Up** at 85' — final push, go all-in
7. If still 0-1 at 88' → GK goes up for corners, waste nothing

### Scenario: Opponent Parked the Bus (0-0, They Won't Come Out)
1. Don't panic-shout — patience is key
2. Add "Work Ball Into Box" and "Lower Tempo" to draw them out
3. Shout: **Encourage** — keep the team calm and patient
4. Look for overloads: bring a full-back into midfield, create a 3v2 wide
5. If still 0-0 at 70' → change a BBM to Mezzala or AP for more creativity
6. Sub: bring on a Target Forward for crosses if your wide players are crossing
7. Shout: **Demand More** at 80' — time to force the issue

### Scenario: Match Pointless? (Already Qualified / Nothing to Play For)
- Rest key players entirely
- Give U21 prospects 45+ minutes
- Test a formation variant you've been considering
- Shout: "No Pressure" — let them play freely

## Part 5: Half-Time Team Talk Integration

Shouts and team talks work together:

| Match Situation | Half-Time Talk | Follow-Up Shout at 55' |
|----------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Winning comfortably | Praise (assertive) | Focus |
| Winning narrowly | "Don't get complacent" | Focus |
| Drawing, should win | "Show me more" (assertive) | Demand More |
| Drawing, fair result | Encourage | Encourage |
| Losing, poor performance | "Not good enough" (assertive) | Demand More |
| Losing, unlucky | "Unlucky" / Encourage | Encourage |

## Checklist: Match Day Routine

- [ ] Pre-match: Check opponent formation, key players, and cards-per-game for the referee
- [ ] 0-15': Observe, no shouts
- [ ] 15-30': First shout if needed (Encourage / Demand More)
- [ ] 30': Check match stats — identify the main issue
- [ ] Half-time: Appropriate team talk
- [ ] 55-65': First substitution (fatigue or tactical)
- [ ] 60': Re-check stats, adjust if needed
- [ ] 70': Second sub, possible mentality shift
- [ ] 80': Third sub, final instructions
- [ ] Post-match: Appropriate team talk, check player ratings and fatigue
