## The FM26 Training Revolution

FM26 changed training fundamentally: you no longer directly control B-team or youth team training. This means your approach to developing young players must shift — **loans and individual training now carry more weight** than ever.

This guide distills the community-tested training framework into an actionable system you can implement today.

---

## Part 1: Coaching Staff Matters More Than Ever

In FM26, the **quality of your staff** directly determines how fast your players grow.

| Staff Role | What They Affect |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Coaches** | Quality of sessions — directly influences attribute growth rate |
| **Sport Scientists** | Injury reduction, fatigue management, match readiness |
| **Physiotherapists** | Recovery speed, accurate injury prognosis feedback |

> **Rule**: Monitor training workload at all times. Knob that goes above "Average" lowers growth rate and increases injuries.

## Part 2: Squad Structure for Optimal Development

### First Team
- **25 players max**
- **2 players per position** + 3 mentors (one defender, midfielder, forward)
- This keeps everyone match-fit without logjams

### Reserves / Youth Teams
- Light management: **15-18 players** — prioritizes playing time
- Detailed management: **22 players max** — two-deep at every position, alternate matches

### Age Tiers
| Age | Primary Growth Driver | Key Action |
|-----|----------------------|------------|
| **15-18** | Training sessions | Don't worry about match ratings; focus on physical attributes |
| **18+** | Matches + match ratings | Loans with guaranteed playing time become essential |
| **First Team** | 15+ minutes on pitch | Only needs 15 minutes to get a match rating and trigger growth |

## Part 3: Individual Training (The 3-Step Method)

### Step 1: Set Injury Risk Control
`Training → Intensity → Reduce injury risk`

### Step 2: Auto-Intensity
Set every player's intensity level to **"Automatic"** — the AI adjusts based on fatigue and match load.

### Step 3: Assign Specific Position / Role / Duty
This is the single most impactful training decision:
- Set **both In-Possession and Out-of-Possession roles** for every player
- Assign a **specific role** (not just a generic position)
- Example: "Mezzala (Support)" not "CM"

### Monthly Feedback Loop
- Training rating **7.5+** → Praise the player
- Training rating **≤6.5** → Criticize
- This directly affects morale and development rate

## Part 4: Season-Phase Training Calendar

### Pre-Season (Weeks 1-4)
**Focus**: Physical conditioning + Team cohesion

- Schedule one friendly per week (every Saturday)
- Starters play 45-60 min, then **sub entire bench** for second half
- Training sessions: heavy on Physical + Tactical blocks
- Add "Team Bonding" and "Match Practice" sessions for cohesion

### Regular Season
**Alternate between Attack weeks and Defense weeks**:
- Attack week: sessions favoring attacking movement, chance creation, set piece offense
- Defense week: sessions favoring defensive shape, pressing triggers, set piece defense
- Rotate weekly to cover all match scenarios without overloading

### Congested Fixture Periods
- Drop intensity, prioritize **Recovery** and **Match Preview**
- Manual "Match Review" session the day after each match
- Rely on the **Rest** function — orange/red fatigue warnings = skip training

### Free Weeks (No Midweek Match)
- Use a structured "Saturday" schedule with balanced sessions
- After a Sunday match: manually add "Match Review" the day after

## Part 5: The 10 Training Commandments

1. **Good coaches** measurably accelerate development — invest here first
2. Always train **specific position/role**, never generic
3. **Pre-season** is the most critical training window — don't skip it
4. Keep training intensity **at or below Medium**
5. Peak intensity **2 days after matchday**, then taper down
6. **Physical attributes** grow fastest; **Mental attributes** slowest
7. Put the **right player on the right squad** (age-appropriate development)
8. **Only promote when they'll actually play** — better to leave in U21 with games
9. Max **2 players per position** in any squad
10. **Consistency** beats perfection — stick with the plan
