## Welcome to FM26 Tactics

Football Manager 2026's tactical system is the deepest it's ever been — and that can be overwhelming. This guide will take you from "what does all this mean?" to "I know exactly how I want to play."

## The Three Pillars of FM26 Tactics

Every tactic in FM26 rests on three foundations:

### 1. Formation
Your team's basic shape — where players stand. The formation is your **defensive shape**. When you lose the ball, this is where your players return to.

Common starting formations for beginners:
- **4-4-2**: Classic, balanced, easy to understand
- **4-2-3-1**: Modern, attacking, flexible
- **4-3-3**: Control midfield, versatile attacking patterns

### 2. Player Roles
What each individual player does within the formation. A "Central Midfielder" and a "Box-to-Box Midfielder" start in the same position but behave completely differently.

**Key concept**: A player's role + duty determines their behavior in ALL phases of play — attacking, defending, and transitioning.

### 3. Team Instructions
How your entire team plays as a unit. These are divided into three phases:
- **In Possession**: What you do when you have the ball
- **In Transition**: What you do when possession changes hands
- **Out of Possession**: What you do when defending

## Step-by-Step: Building Your First Tactic

### Step 1: Choose Your Playing Style

Before touching a single instruction, decide HOW you want to play:

| Playing Style | Key Idea | Good For |
|--------------|----------|----------|
| **Gegenpress** | Win the ball back high up the pitch | Teams with high work rate and stamina |
| **Possession** | Keep the ball, control the game | Technically superior teams |
| **Counter-Attack** | Defend deep, break quickly | Underdogs, teams with pace |
| **Wing Play** | Cross from wide areas | Teams with tall strikers and good wingers |
| **Direct/Route One** | Get the ball forward quickly | Physical teams, lower leagues |

### Step 2: Pick a Formation That Matches

- Want to press high? → 4-2-3-1, 4-3-3
- Want to counter? → 4-4-2, 3-5-2
- Want to control possession? → 4-3-3, 3-4-3
- Just starting out? → 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1

### Step 3: Assign Player Roles

Every role has a specific behavior. For beginners, follow this rule:

> **One holder, one runner, one creator in midfield.**

Example for 4-3-3:
- DM/CM (Defend) = holder (sits, screens defense)
- CM/B2B (Support) = runner (box-to-box energy)
- CM/AP (Support) = creator (links midfield to attack)

### Step 4: Set Team Instructions Wisely

**Start simple.** The most common beginner mistake is selecting too many instructions. Start with 3-5 instructions and add more as you understand how your tactic plays.

Bare minimum for any tactic:
1. **In Possession**: Attacking width
2. **In Transition**: Counter or counter-press? (pick one)
3. **Out of Possession**: Defensive line + line of engagement

### Step 5: Test and Adjust

Watch the first 15 minutes of every match on "Comprehensive" highlights. Look for:
- Where are you losing the ball?
- Who's getting too much space?
- Are your instructions actually happening on the pitch?

## Understanding Mentality

Mentality affects everything — passing risk, tempo, width, defensive line. Think of it as your overall approach:

| Mentality | When to Use |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Very Attacking** | Last 10 minutes, must score |
| **Attacking** | You're the better team and want to dominate |
| **Positive** | Balanced but proactive (most common) |
| **Balanced** | Even matchups, feeling out the game |
| **Cautious** | Protecting a lead, tough away games |
| **Defensive** | Underdog, park the bus |
| **Very Defensive** | Last 5 minutes, one-goal lead |

> **For beginners**: Start on Balanced or Positive. These mentalities give you the most natural football without extreme behaviors.

## The Most Important Thing: Player Suitability

The best tactic in the world will fail with the wrong players. Before committing to a style:

1. Check your squad's **Work Rate** and **Stamina** averages (Comparison → General)
2. Check your squad's **Passing** and **First Touch** (Comparison → Technical)
3. Check your squad's **Pace** and **Acceleration** (Comparison → Physical)

If your team has low work rate, don't play gegenpress. If your team has low passing, don't play possession. Match the tactic to your players, not the other way around.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Too many team instructions**: Start with 3-5, add gradually
2. **Ignoring player attributes**: Roles need specific attributes to work
3. **All Attack duties**: You need balance — some players must stay back
4. **Changing tactics every match**: Give systems 5-8 games to bed in
5. **No plan B**: Have a second tactic trained for different situations
