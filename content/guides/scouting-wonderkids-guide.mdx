## Stop Chasing, Start Attracting

In FM26, waiting for scout reports means you're always second. The clubs that dominate the transfer market don't react to talent — they intercept it before anyone else notices.

This guide covers the complete recruitment playbook: automated systems, manual hunting techniques, and FM26's new TransferRoom tool.

---

## Part 1: The Automated Pipeline — Let Your Staff Do the Work

### Delegate Youth Recruitment to Your Director of Football

If you have a DoF with high **Judging Player Potential** (18+) who prefers signing "young players for the future," delegate smartly:

1. **Staff → Responsibilities → Transfers & Contracts**
2. Assign **"Finds and makes offers for young players for the U19 squad"** to your DoF
3. **Retain "Finalizes signings"** — you want the veto power

**Best DoF Candidate Traits**:
- Judging Player Potential: 18+
- Judging Player Ability: 15+
- Preferred transfer policy: "Signs young players for the future"
- Preferred formation: Matches your system (squad fit)

Your DoF will periodically present 15-18 year olds they've scouted and negotiated for. Review the scout report, check personality, and decide — this alone can feed your academy pipeline with zero effort.

### Set Up Ongoing Recruitment Focuses

Navigate to **Scouting → Recruitment Focuses → Create New Focus**:

#### Focus #1: The Wonderkid Trap
- **Position**: Any (talent over position at young ages)
- **Age**: 15-21
- **Current Ability**: 1.5 stars minimum
- **Potential Ability**: 3.5-4 stars minimum (at your club's level)
- **Region**: Based on your scouting budget. Priority: South America (value), Eastern Europe (physical), Scandinavia (bargains)
- **Priority**: Ongoing
- **Advanced**: Check "EU Nationality" if you face non-EU restrictions. Check "Homegrown (15-21)" to build Champions League squad compliance.

#### Focus #2: The Expiring Contract Raid
- **Contract**: Expiring in 6-12 months
- **Age**: 18-28
- **Current Ability**: 2.5 stars minimum
- **League**: Same division or higher
- Pick up quality players on free transfers — squad depth for zero cost.

#### Focus #3: The Unhappy Bargain Bin
- **Transfer Status**: Listed or Unhappy
- **Age**: 18-26
- **Current Ability**: 2.5 stars minimum
- Players available below market value because of squad status issues.

## Part 2: Youth Intake Interception — Manual Hunting

This technique bypasses scouting entirely. You see every newgen the moment they're generated.

### Step-by-Step: Youth Intake Day Calendar

1. Note the **Youth Intake dates** by country:
   - England: Late February
   - Spain: Late March
   - Germany: Early March
   - France: Early March
   - Brazil: Late September
   - Argentina: Late August
   - *Check online for the full calendar by country*

2. On intake day, click **World** icon (top-right) → Select region

3. Navigate to **Transfers** tab → Dropdown → **Youth Intake**

4. Filter by the relevant month and country

5. You'll see every newgen generated that cycle. Sort by value, scout, or **invite for trial** (zero cost)

### Why Trials Are OP
Inviting for a trial is **free** and reveals accurate attributes immediately — no scouting budget needed, no 2-week wait. For lower-league teams, this is how you find the gems that don't show up on any report.

## Part 3: Data-Driven Scouting — Follow the Performances

Stop relying on scout opinions alone. Dig into real match data:

### Target: UEFA Youth League
- The U19 equivalent of the Champions League
- **Filter**: Players 16-17 years old (performing above their age bracket)
- Sort by key stats: Goals, Assists, Pass Completion%, Tackles Won, Key Passes
- A 16-year-old with 5 goals in 6 U19 CL matches = investigate immediately

### Target: International Youth Tournaments
- U20 World Cup
- U21 European Championship
- U20 African Cup of Nations
- U20 South American Championship

Watch the knockout stages especially — players who perform in pressure situations reveal high Important Matches hidden attribute.

### The "Match" Search Technique

In player search, don't use "AND" for all attribute filters — you'll return zero results. Instead:

- Set core DNA attributes (Acceleration, Determination, Work Rate, Technique) at **12-13 minimum**
- Use **"Match at least X of Y conditions"** — e.g. "Match at least 4 of 6"
- This finds raw talents who may have one weak area but elite potential elsewhere

## Part 4: TransferRoom — FM26's New Recruitment Weapon

TransferRoom lets you **broadcast your requirements** to every AI club and agent simultaneously.

### Wonderkid-Specific TransferRoom Setup

1. Navigate to **Transfers → TransferRoom**
2. Create a new Opportunity:
   - **Age**: 15-21
   - **Transfer Type**: Transfer
   - **Playing Time**: **Future Prospect** (this is key — ensures you only get prospects willing to develop)
   - Position: Any or specific needs
3. Submit and wait

The system filters out players demanding "Important Player" status or high wages — you'll receive notifications only about affordable young talents ready to develop in your U21/reserve setup.

## Part 5: The Complete Recruitment Cycle

### Weekly Routine
- **Monday**: Review Recruitment Focus results (auto-filtered prospects)
- **Mid-week**: Scout any flagged players from UYL/U20 tournaments
- **Weekend**: Play matches — track opposition youngsters who impress

### Monthly Routine
- Review DoF recommendations for youth signings
- Check TransferRoom responses — respond within 24 hours
- Update recruitment focuses if current ones are returning dead ends
- Review your wonderkid shortlist — remove players who've moved or stalled

### Youth Intake Season (Per Country)
- Day before: Clear your shortlist of "trial" players
- Intake day: Scour the list, invite trials for top 20
- Within 1 week: Decide on 3-5 approaches, sign 1-3

### Off-Season
- Review all U21/U18 players — promote, loan, or sell
- Evaluate your recruitment for the year: which method found the best players?
- Adjust budget allocation: more for South America? More for domestic youth competitions?

## The 5 Golden Rules of FM26 Recruitment

1. **Speed kills** — the first club to offer usually wins the wonderkid race
2. **Trials are free** — invite liberally, sign sparingly
3. **Personality > Ability** at youth level — a 3-star with Professional personality develops faster than a 4-star with Slack
4. **EU nationality saves squad slots** — filter for it early
5. **Don't hoard** — 25-man first team max, 18-man youth teams. More than that and playing time evaporates
