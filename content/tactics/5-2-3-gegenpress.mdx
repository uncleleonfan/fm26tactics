# 5-2-3 Gegenpress: The Complete FM26 Guide

The 5-2-3 Gegenpress combines the defensive solidity of five at the back with the most aggressive pressing system in Football Manager 2026. The wing-backs provide width in both phases, the dual pivot shields the back five, and the front three presses relentlessly. This guide covers the complete system.

## Part 1: Tactical Philosophy

### What the 5-2-3 Gegenpress Does in FM26

- **Phase 1 — The Trap** (out of possession): A high line with the front three pressing the opposition CBs. The dual pivot cuts off passing lanes to the midfield. The wing-backs push up to press the opposition wingers. The opposition is forced into long balls — which your three CBs win aerially.

- **Phase 2 — The Press** (transition): The moment the ball is lost, every player sprints to press the nearest opponent. The 5-2-3 shape allows aggressive pressing because five CBs provide a safety net behind the press.

- **Phase 3 — The Overload** (attacking): Wing-backs push up to the winger position, creating a 3-4-3 in attack. The dual 10s (IFs) operate in the half-spaces, and the CF leads the line. Five players attack with width and creativity.

## Part 2: Team Instructions

### In Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Attacking Width** | Fairly Wide | WBs provide width. IFs operate in half-spaces. |
| **Passing Directness** | Slightly Short | Gegenpress wins the ball high — keep it with short passes. |
| **Tempo** | Higher | Quick circulation catches the defence unset. |
| **Play Out of Defence** | ON | The CBs are comfortable on the ball — start from the back. |
| **Pass Into Space** | ON | The IFs and CF make runs behind the defence. |
| **Run At Defence** | ON | The IFs should dribble at the CBs after winning the ball high. |
| **Work Ball Into Box** | ON | Patient build-up after the initial counter chance. |

### In Transition

| Instruction | Setting | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Counter** | ON | The front three is already high — quick counters from turnovers. |
| **Counter-Press** | ON | The defining instruction. Press immediately after losing the ball. |
| **GK Distribution** | Roll Out to CBs | Start build-up from the back. |

### Out of Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Defensive Line** | Much Higher | Compress space — the press starts high up the pitch. |
| **Line of Engagement** | High Block | Start pressing in the attacking third. |
| **Trigger Press** | Much More Often | Every player presses immediately. |
| **Defensive Width** | Standard | The WBs cover the flanks, CBs cover the centre. |
| **Offside Trap** | ON | High line with fast CBs catches strikers offside. |
| **Get Stuck In** | ON | Aggressive tackling wins the ball back faster. |

## Part 3: Player Roles

### Goalkeeper: Sweeper Keeper (Support)

```
Must-have: Reflexes 14+, Kicking 14+, Passing 13+, One on Ones 14+
PPMs: "Uses Long Throws to Start Counter Attacks"
```

The SK sweeps behind the high line and starts build-up with short passes. Rushing out to clear long balls over the top is critical — One on Ones 14+ helps in 1v1 situations.

### Centre-Backs: The Back Five

#### Central CB: CD (Cover)

```
Must-have: Positioning 15+, Anticipation 15+, Pace 14+
PPMs: "Stays Back at All Times"
```

The sweeper. Sits 3-5 yards deeper to cover the high line. Must have good Pace to recover if the offside trap fails.

#### Outside CBs: CD (Defend) × 4

```
Must-have: Marking 14+, Tackling 14+, Pace 13+, Jumping Reach 14+
PPMs: "Marks Opponent Tightly"
```

Four disciplined defenders. With the high line, Pace 13+ is essential — slow CBs will be exploited by pacey strikers.

### Wing-Backs: The Pressing Engine

#### Wing-Back (Attack) × 2

```
Must-have: Stamina 16+, Pace 14+, Crossing 14+, Work Rate 16+
PPMs: "Gets Forward Whenever Possible", "Hugs Line"
```

The WBs press the opposition wingers high up the pitch, then sprint forward to provide width in attack. Elite Work Rate and Stamina are non-negotiable. Sub at 60-65 minutes.

> **Variant**: Facing a narrow midfield (e.g. a diamond), switch the right WB to a **Playmaking Wing-Back (Attack)**. He starts wide to hold the width, then drifts inside to create a midfield overload alongside the DLP — extra passing lanes without losing the out ball. Requires Passing 15+ on top of the WB attributes.

### Midfield: The Double Pivot

#### Deep-Lying Playmaker (Defend)

```
Must-have: Passing 15+, Vision 15+, Composure 14+, Decisions 14+
PPMs: "Dictates Tempo", "Tries Killer Balls Often"
```

The primary creator. Sits at the base of the midfield, receives from the CBs, and launches through balls to the IFs or CF. The DLP is the brain of the system.

#### Pressing Central Midfielder (Defend)

```
Must-have: Tackling 15+, Work Rate 16+, Stamina 16+, Aggression 15+
PPMs: "Dives Into Tackles", "Stays Back at All Times"
```

The pressing machine. Presses aggressively in the midfield, wins the ball back, and lays off to the DLP. Work Rate 16+ and Stamina 16+ are essential for the gegenpress.

### The Front Three: The Pressing Unit

#### Inside Forward (Attack) × 2

```
Must-have: Dribbling 14+, Pace 15+, Technique 14+, Off The Ball 14+
PPMs: "Cuts Inside", "Runs with Ball Through Centre", "Places Shots"
```

The IFs press the opposition CBs and FBs, then cut inside to create scoring chances. On Attack duty to maximize the counter-attacking threat after winning the ball high.

#### Centre Forward (Attack)

```
Must-have: Pace 15+, Off The Ball 15+, Finishing 14+, Work Rate 14+
PPMs: "Moves Into Channels", "Places Shots", "Likes to Beat Offside Trap"
```

The CF leads the press from the front. He presses the opposition CBs, forces errors, and finishes the chances created by the IFs. Work Rate 14+ is needed for the pressing role.

## Part 4: Set Piece Integration

### Defensive Corners

Three CBs in the 6-yard box. The PCM marks the edge for counter-press. One IF stays forward as a counter threat. WBs mark wide areas.

### Attacking Corners

The CF attacks near post. One CB attacks far post. The DLP takes the corner — aim near post, in-swinging. WBs stay back for defensive cover.

## Part 6: In-Match Adjustments

### Chasing a Goal at Minute 75

- Push mentality to **Attacking**
- Switch one IF to **Attack** (already there) and raise WBs to **Attack**
- Replace the PCM with a second CF (switch to 3-2-4-1 — all-out attack)

### Protecting a Lead at Minute 80

- Drop mentality to **Cautious**
- Drop WBs to **Support** duty (back five becomes permanent)
- Replace one IF with a fresh PCM (switch to 5-3-1-1)

## Part 7: Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths

| Strength | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Aggressive pressing** | 5-2-3 allows the most aggressive pressing in the game — five CBs provide a safety net. |
| **Defensive solidity** | Even with the high line, five CBs + two DMs = 7 defenders. |
| **Wing-back overloads** | WBs create 2v1s on the flanks every match. |
| **High-line recovery** | Three CBs with Pace 13+ can recover if the offside trap fails. |

### Weaknesses

| Weakness | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Stamina intensive** | The gegenpress drains stamina fast — need 3-4 subs per match. |
| **High line vulnerability** | Pace 14+ strikers can exploit the space behind. Use fast CBs. |
| **Width dependency** | If WBs are pinned back, the attack becomes too narrow. Keep backup WBs fresh. |
| **Dual pivot overload** | Two DMs can be overwhelmed by three-man midfields (4-3-3). Use the IFs to track back. |

## Part 9: FAQ

**Q: How is the 5-2-3 different from the 4-2-3-1 Gegenpress?**
A: The 4-2-3-1 Gegenpress (see [4-2-3-1 Gegenpress](/tactics/4-2-3-1-gegenpress)) uses a back four with one striker. The 5-2-3 uses a back five with wing-backs — more defensive security but less central creativity. The 5-2-3 is better for teams with elite wing-backs.

**Q: Why five at the back for gegenpress?**
A: The high line of the gegenpress is risky. Five CBs provide a safety net — even if the press is beaten, there are three CBs to cover. The 5-2-3 allows the most aggressive pressing with the least risk.

**Q: Do I need fast centre-backs?**
A: Yes. With a Much Higher defensive line, Pace 13+ is the minimum for CBs. Slow CBs (Pace < 12) will be exposed by pacey strikers playing long balls over the top.
