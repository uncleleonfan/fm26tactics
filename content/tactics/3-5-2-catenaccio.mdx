## Why Catenaccio?

Catenaccio — Italian for "door bolt" — is the original defensive masterclass. In FM26, when you're managing an underdog, fighting relegation, or protecting a lead against superior opposition, nothing beats a well-drilled 3-5-2 defensive block.

The system creates a back five out of possession that's incredibly difficult to break down, while the two strikers provide a constant counter-attacking threat. It's the ultimate tool for punching above your weight.

## Team Instructions

### In Possession

- Attacking width: **Narrow** — stay compact, play through the middle
- Passing directness: **More direct**
- Tempo: **Higher** — no time-wasting, hit them on the break
- Play out of defense: **Leave disabled** — clear your lines under pressure
- Pass into space: **Enabled**
- Run at defense: **Enabled**

### In Transition

- Counter: **Enabled** — this is your primary attacking weapon
- Counter-press: **Leave disabled** — retreat into shape immediately
- Distribute to target forward: **Enabled**
- Distribute quickly: **Enabled** — launch counters before they reset

### Out of Possession

- Line of engagement: **Low block**
- Defensive line: **Much lower**
- Trigger press: **Much less often**
- Prevent short GK distribution: **Leave disabled**
- Get stuck in: **Enabled** — you want physicality in the tackle
- Offside trap: **Leave disabled** — deep line makes this dangerous
- Defensive width: **Force opposition outside** — aerial dominance in the box

## Player Roles

### Goalkeeper
- **Goalkeeper (Defend)** — Pure shot-stopper, doesn't need distribution skills

### Defense
- **Central Defender (Cover)** — Center of the three, sweeps up through balls
- **Central Defender (Stopper)** — Right side, aggressively engages attackers
- **Central Defender (Stopper)** — Left side, mirrors the right

### Midfield
- **Complete Wing-Back (Support)** — Right side: entire flank responsibility
- **Ball-Winning Midfielder (Defend)** — Destroys attacks, simple passes forward
- **Deep-Lying Playmaker (Support)** — Launches counters from deep with long passes
- **Ball-Winning Midfielder (Defend)** — Second destroyer, shields the back three
- **Complete Wing-Back (Support)** — Left side: must have elite stamina

### Attack
- **Target Forward (Attack)** — Aerial outlet, holds up long clearances
- **Advanced Forward (Attack)** — Speed merchant, runs channels on the break

> **Pro Tip**: Your wing-backs are the most important players in this formation. They must contribute defensively in a back 5 AND provide the only width going forward. Stamina, Work Rate, and Crossing are non-negotiable.

## Player Attributes

For catenaccio to work, you need specific profiles:

1. **Positioning** (Minimum 14 for all defenders) — Holding the defensive line
2. **Jumping Reach** (Minimum 15 for center-backs) — Aerial dominance in the box
3. **Pace & Acceleration** (Minimum 15 for Advanced Forward) — Counter-attacking weapon
4. **Stamina** (Minimum 15 for wing-backs) — 90 minutes of flank-to-flank running
5. **Tackling** (Minimum 14 for BWM) — Must win the ball cleanly
6. **Passing & Vision** (Minimum 13 for DLP) — Launches the counter-attack

## Common Mistakes

### Pushing the defensive line too high
Catenaccio only works with a deep block. If you push up, you expose the space behind your wing-backs — the exact space this system is designed to deny.

### Playing short passes out of defense
Direct distribution is essential. Short passing invites the opposition press and can lead to turnovers in dangerous areas. Clear first, ask questions later.

### Wrong striker partnership
You need one physical outlet AND one speedster. Two Target Forwards lack the pace to counter, while two Advanced Forwards will never win the long clearances that start your breaks.

## When to Use Catenaccio

- Away from home against superior opposition
- Protecting a lead in the final 20-30 minutes
- Managing a relegation-threatened side
- Champions League knockout away legs
- Against teams that commit many players forward (4-2-4, 4-2-3-1 Gegenpress)

## Match Adjustments

| Situation | Adjustment |
|-----------|------------|
| Can't get out of own half | Increase passing directness to maximum, use "Hit Early Crosses" |
| Conceding from crosses | Switch defensive width to "Force opposition inside" |
| Need a goal | Push wing-backs to Attack duty, raise line of engagement one notch |
| Early red card | Remove a striker, switch to 5-3-1 with defensive wingers |

## Conclusion

Catenaccio isn't pretty — but it wins football matches. In FM26, a properly set up 3-5-2 defensive system is one of the most reliable ways to overachieve with limited resources. Embrace the dark arts, frustrate your opponents, and let the counter-attacks do the talking.
