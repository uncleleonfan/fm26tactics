## Why Counter-Attack 3-5-2?

When you're predicted to finish mid-table or below, you need a system that absorbs pressure and strikes with precision. The 3-5-2 provides:

- **Three center-backs** for aerial dominance
- **Two strikers** to exploit counter-attack opportunities
- **Midfield overload** to win the ball centrally

## Team Instructions

### In Possession
- Attacking width: **Fairly wide** (stretch on the break)
- Passing directness: **More direct**
- Tempo: **Much higher tempo**
- Hit early crosses: **Enabled**

### In Transition
- Counter: **Enabled** (absolutely crucial)
- Regroup: **Enabled**
- Distribute quickly: **Enabled**
- Distribute to target forward: **Enabled**

### Out of Possession
- Line of engagement: **Lower**
- Defensive line: **Standard** (don't drop too deep)
- Trigger press: **Less often**
- Get stuck in: **Enabled**

## Player Roles

- **Goalkeeper (Defend)** — Traditional shot-stopper
- **Centre-Backs (Defend)** ×3 — Solid defensive trio
- **Wing-Backs (Support)** — Provide width, track back diligently
- **Deep-Lying Playmaker (Support)** — Quick transition passes
- **Box-to-Box Midfielder** — Links defense to attack
- **Centre Forward (Attack)** — Stretches the defense
- **Target Forward (Support)** — Hold-up play for counter launches

> **Pro Tip**: The Target Forward is your outlet. When your goalkeeper or defenders win the ball, look for the TF to hold it up while midfielders surge forward.

## Counter-Attacking Patterns

### Pattern 1: Wing-Back Overlap
1. Ball won in central midfield
2. Quick pass to Target Forward who holds up
3. Wing-Back bursts forward on the overlap
4. Early cross to the Centre Forward

### Pattern 2: Direct Through Ball
1. Defensive interception
2. Deep-Lying Playmaker spots the run
3. Centre Forward makes a diagonal run
4. Through ball over the top

## When to Switch

Counter-attack works best against:
- Teams that press high (e.g., Liverpool-style gegenpress)
- Teams playing 4-2-3-1 or 4-3-3 with attacking full-backs
- Opponents you're expected to lose against

Switch to a more proactive style against:
- Teams that also sit deep (will result in a stalemate)
- Relegation candidates parking the bus
- When you need a goal in the final 20 minutes

The 3-5-2 counter-attack is FM26's ultimate giant-killer formation. Master it, and you'll punch well above your weight.
