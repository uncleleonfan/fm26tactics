# 4-2-4 Attacking: The Complete FM26 Guide

The 4-2-4 is the nuclear option in Football Manager 2026 — four forwards, two midfielders, and a direct route-one style. It's not a formation for 90 minutes. It's the desperation tactic for when you need a goal in the last 15 minutes, or the ultra-aggressive choice for teams that want to overwhelm the opposition with pure attacking force.

## Part 1: Tactical Philosophy

### What the 4-2-4 Attacking Does in FM26

- **Phase 1 — The Bombardment** (in possession): Four forwards flood the box. Two wingers stay wide for crosses, a Target Forward attacks the near post, and an Advanced Forward attacks the far post. The two CMs feed the ball forward relentlessly.

- **Phase 2 — The Route** (build-up): The ball goes direct — long passes from the CMs or the GK to the TF. No patient build-up. No sideways passing. Vertical urgency is the entire strategy.

- **Phase 3 — The Recovery** (out of possession): The 4-2-4 has only two midfielders and four defenders protecting the goal. The opposition has vast space in midfield to exploit. This is accepted risk — you're trading defensive solidity for attacking power.

### Formation Shape

```
   W(A)    TF(A)              AF(A)    W(A)

               BWM(D)    DLP(S)

   FB(A)    CD(D)    CD(D)    FB(A)

                        SK(S)
```

> **Key**: Four forwards, two midfielders, four defenders. The 4-2-4 is the most top-heavy formation in the game — maximum attacking output, minimum defensive cover.

## Part 2: Team Instructions

### In Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Attacking Width** | Wide | Wingers need maximum width to stretch the defence and create crossing lanes. |
| **Passing Directness** | Much More Direct | Every pass goes forward. Route One execution. |
| **Tempo** | Much Higher | Maximum urgency — the clock is ticking. |
| **Play Out of Defence** | OFF | Never. The ball goes long immediately. |
| **Pass Into Space** | ON | The AF and Ws make runs behind the defence. |
| **Hit Early Crosses** | ON | Wingers cross from deep — don't wait for overlaps. |
| **Work Ball Into Box** | OFF | Shoot from anywhere. Cross from anywhere. |
| **Shoot on Sight** | ON | Any shot is a good shot when chasing a goal. |

### In Transition

| Instruction | Setting | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Counter** | ON | The four forwards are already high — quick counters. |
| **Counter-Press** | OFF | Can't counter-press with only two midfielders. Retreat. |
| **GK Distribution** | Long Kick to TF | Skip the build-up. |
| **Distribute Quickly** | ON | Release before the opposition resets. |

### Out of Possession

| Instruction | Setting | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Defensive Line** | Higher | Compress space — but with only two CMs, the midfield is thin. |
| **Line of Engagement** | Mid Block | Start pressing in the middle third. |
| **Trigger Press** | Standard | Can't press aggressively with four forwards committed. |
| **Defensive Width** | Standard | FBs cover the flanks, CD cover the centre. |
| **Offside Trap** | ON | High line catches long balls. |
| **Get Stuck In** | ON | Win the ball back quickly in midfield. |

## Part 3: Player Roles

### Goalkeeper: Sweeper Keeper (Support)

```
Must-have: Reflexes 14+, Kicking 14+, Passing 13+, One on Ones 14+
PPMs: "Uses Long Throws to Start Counter Attacks"
```

The SK starts attacks with long kicks to the TF and sweeps behind the high line. With only two CMs protecting, the GK is the last line of defence — One on Ones 14+ is critical.

### Centre-Backs: CD (Defend) × 2

```
Must-have: Marking 14+, Tackling 14+, Positioning 14+, Pace 13+
PPMs: "Stays Back at All Times"
```

Two disciplined defenders. They hold the line and clear long. Pace 13+ is needed for the high line — slow CBs will be exploited.

### Full-Backs: FB (Attack) × 2

```
Must-have: Stamina 15+, Crossing 14+, Pace 14+, Work Rate 14+
PPMs: "Gets Forward Whenever Possible", "Runs with Ball Down Left/Right"
```

The FBs push up to provide width — with four forwards committed, the FBs are the only defensive-wide cover. On Attack duty to overlap the wingers and create 2v1s on the flanks. High Stamina is essential.

### Midfield: The Bare Minimum

#### Ball-Winning Midfielder (Defend)

```
Must-have: Tackling 15+, Work Rate 16+, Stamina 16+, Aggression 14+
PPMs: "Stays Back at All Times", "Dives Into Tackles"
```

The only true defensive player in the midfield. He sits in front of the CBs, wins the ball, and lays off to the DLP. Never goes forward — he's the shield for the back four.

#### Deep-Lying Playmaker (Support)

```
Must-have: Passing 15+, Vision 15+, Composure 14+, Long Shots 13+
PPMs: "Tries Killer Balls Often", "Switches Ball to Other Flank"
```

The launchpad. Receives from the BWM and hits long balls to the TF or through balls to the wingers. On Support duty to maintain a midfield presence — if the DLP goes forward, there's no one in midfield.

### The Front Four: The Nuclear Arsenal

#### Winger (Attack) × 2

```
Must-have: Pace 15+, Crossing 15+, Dribbling 14+, Technique 14+
PPMs: "Runs with Ball Down Left/Right", "Hits Early Crosses"
```

The wingers stay wide, run at the FBs, and cross early. They're the primary supply line for the TF and AF. On Attack duty — no defensive responsibilities.

#### Target Forward (Attack)

```
Must-have: Jumping Reach 16+, Strength 15+, Heading 14+, First Touch 13+
PPMs: "Plays With Back to Goal", "Knocks Ball Past Opponent"
```

The focal point. He receives every long ball, holds off the CB, and lays off to the AF or wingers. His aerial dominance is the key to the 4-2-4 — he wins 30+ headers per match.

#### Advanced Forward (Attack)

```
Must-have: Pace 16+, Off The Ball 15+, Finishing 14+, Composure 13+
PPMs: "Moves Into Channels", "Places Shots"
```

The finisher. He feeds off the TF's knockdowns and the wingers' crosses. His pace gets him into the box before the defence recovers.

## Part 4: Set Piece Integration

### Defensive Corners

Both CBs and the BWM in the 6-yard box. The DLP marks the edge for counter-launch. The TF stays forward as a counter threat. One winger stays back.

### Attacking Corners

The TF attacks near post, the AF far post. Both CBs attack the keeper. One winger takes the corner — aim near post, in-swinging. The other winger stays back.

Press the CBs and DMs to force turnovers in the opposition's build-up — with four forwards ready to counter, any turnover in the opposition half is a chance.

## Part 6: In-Match Adjustments

### This IS the Adjustment

The 4-2-4 is typically used as an in-match adjustment, not a starting formation. Use it when:

- **Losing by 1-2 goals at minute 75+**: Switch from your base formation to 4-2-4
- **Need to score against a low block**: Flood the box with four forwards
- **Opposition has 10 men**: Overload their defensive structure

### If Already in 4-2-4

**Need MORE goals (minute 85+):**
- Push mentality to **Very Attacking**
- Replace the BWM with a third forward (switch to 3-1-6 — all-out attack)
- Push both FBs to **Attack**
- Remove all defensive instructions

**Need to protect (minute 90+):**
- Drop mentality to **Defensive**
- Drop wingers to **Defend** duty
- Replace one winger with a CB (switch to 5-2-2-1)

## Part 7: Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths

| Strength | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Maximum attack** | Four forwards = the most attacking power possible. |
| **Aerial dominance** | TF + two CBs attacking corners = 3 aerial threats. |
| **Wide overloads** | Wingers + overlapping FBs = 2v1s on both flanks. |
| **Comeback potential** | The best formation for chasing a goal in the last 15 minutes. |

### Weaknesses

| Weakness | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Midfield exposure** | Only two CMs — vast space for the opposition. Accept the risk. |
| **Counter vulnerability** | Four forwards committed = 4 players not defending. Need pacey CBs. |
| **Not a 90-minute system** | Stamina drains fast with four forwards pressing. Use for 15-20 minutes max. |
| **Low possession** | The direct style means you'll have 35-40% possession. That's fine — it's about goals, not possession. |

## Part 9: FAQ

**Q: Should I start matches with the 4-2-4?**
A: No. The 4-2-4 is too risky for 90 minutes — only two midfielders means you'll be overrun in the centre. Start with a balanced formation (4-2-3-1 or 4-3-3) and switch to 4-2-4 when you need a goal. See our [4-2-3-1 Gegenpress](/tactics/4-2-3-1-gegenpress) or [4-3-3 Tiki-Taka](/tactics/4-3-3-tiki-taka) for starting formations.

**Q: Why Route One and not a more creative style?**
A: The 4-2-4 is about quantity of chances, not quality. Route One gets the ball forward fast — every long ball to the TF is a potential goal. Creative styles require time and possession, which the 4-2-4 doesn't have.

**Q: What if the opposition counters?**
A: It will happen. The 4-2-4 concedes counters — that's the trade-off. Use pacey CBs (Pace 13+) and accept that you'll concede some goals. The goal is to score MORE than you concede.
